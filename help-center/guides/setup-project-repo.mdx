---

## Connect Your Repo

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Projects">
    In the Percepteye dashboard, go to Projects.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Repository">
    Click "Add Repo" and select your version control provider (GitHub, GitLab).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Access">
    Grant Percepteye permission to access your repository.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    Your repo should appear in the Projects list.
  </Step>
</Steps>
